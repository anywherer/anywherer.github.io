## 1

Chairs have two prices. One type is 30 dollars more expensive than the other. You can buy 10 more of the cheaper ones than the expensive ones for 1800 dollars. Find the cost of each type of chair.

Let the less expensive chair cost $x$ dollars.
The number of cheap chairs you can buy with 1800 dollars is $\dfrac{1800}{x}$.

$1800=(\dfrac{1800}{x}-10)\cdot (x+30)$

$1800=1800+\dfrac{1800\cdot 30}{x}-10x-300$
$0=\dfrac{1800\cdot 30}{x}-10x-300$
$0=1800\cdot 30-10x^2-300x$
$0=x^2+30x-5400$
$0=(x+60)(x-90)$
$x=-60\lor x=90$
Cannot be negative.
$x=90$

## 2

Some identical piggy banks have a total of 2100 dollars. Seven piggy banks disappeared and the rest have 10 dollars added, and after that the total is 2100 dollars. How many piggy banks originally?

Let there be $x$ piggy banks originally.
$(x-7)\cdot (\dfrac{2100}{x}+10)=2100$
$(x-7)\cdot (\dfrac{2100}{x}+10)=2100$
$2100+10x+\dfrac{-7\cdot 2100}{x}-70=2100$
$10x+\dfrac{-7\cdot 2100}{x}-70=0$
$10x^2+-7\cdot 2100-70x=0$
$x^2-7x-1470=0$
$(x-42)(x+35)=0$
$x=42\lor x=-35$
Cannot be negative.
$x=42$
